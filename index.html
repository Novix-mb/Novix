About NOVIX

NOVIX
Think Beyond

NOVIX is your personal study workspace: keep your books, tasks and notes, plan your week and stay focused, all in one calm place.

Library

Upload your PDF books, read them on this device and write on the pages with a pen, highlighter, text, sticky notes and shapes.

My Tasks

Add tasks by difficulty, easiest first, tick them off and watch your progress grow.

Notes

Write and pin notes, format your text and keep every idea organised and easy to find.

Timetable

Plan your week with colour-coded sessions, link them to your tasks and get a reminder when it is time.

Focus Timer

Focus and break sessions with soft sounds, calm backgrounds and a screen that stays awake while the timer runs.

Smart Board

A smart whiteboard to draw, write and present: pens, highlighter, shapes, text, sticky notes, stickers, images and a laser pointer, with many pages you can save and revisit.
